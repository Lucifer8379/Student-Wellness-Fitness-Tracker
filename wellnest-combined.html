Wellness Tips
🧠
Mind-Body Connection
Even 20 minutes of brisk walking can improve focus and memory retention before studying.
💤
Sleep is Non-Negotiable
Aim for 7–9 hours per night with a consistent schedule — even on weekends.
💧
Stay Hydrated
Dehydration of just 2% impairs short-term memory. Aim for 2 litres every day.
🥦
Fuel Smart
Meal prep on Sundays. A well-nourished brain retains more during exams.
🧘
Micro-Mindfulness
Box breathing (4-4-4-4) between study sessions activates calm and reduces cortisol.
📵
Digital Detox
Schedule 1-hour no-screen windows daily for deeper rest and better melatonin.
