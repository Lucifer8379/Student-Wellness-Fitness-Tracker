Student Wellness
& Fitness Tracker

A smart web application to help students track activity, sleep, hydration, wellness scores, goals, reminders, and peer challenges.

Core Features

🏃

Activity Log

Record daily workouts, steps, exercises, and fitness activities to monitor student health progress.

😴

Sleep Tracker

Track sleep duration and quality to encourage better rest and academic performance.

💧

Hydration Monitor

Log water intake and receive reminders to maintain proper hydration throughout the day.

📊

Progress Charts

Display weekly goals, wellness scores, and progress using simple visual charts and reports.

🔔

Notification System

Send reminders for workouts, hydration, sleep goals, and weekly wellness targets.

🤖

WhatsApp Chatbot

Allow students to log data, check progress, and receive wellness updates through WhatsApp.

Project Objective

To improve student health awareness by combining wellness tracking, goal setting, notifications, peer motivation, and chatbot-based accessibility into one easy-to-use platform.
